PRIVATE COLLECTION

Four handcrafted sauces designed to seduce the palate. Tap any bottle to reveal its secrets.

Bite My Peach
Chili • Peach
Sweet temptation followed by a surprisingly assertive finish.
Main Ingredients
Ripe peach, vibrant chili peppers, citrus notes, cane sugar and a carefully balanced spice blend.
Sensation
Velvety sweetness greets the tongue before an intense kick arrives with confidence. The heat builds quickly, then releases into a lingering fruity finish. Intense kick, sudden release — just how I like it.
Heat Level — 6/10
Mango Me Harder
Mango • Horny Habanero
Tropical sweetness with a playful fiery personality.
Main Ingredients
Fresh mango, habanero peppers, tropical fruit concentrates and citrus accents.
Sensation
Rich mango sweetness glides across the palate before the habanero begins its flirtation. Smooth, bright, seductive and dangerously inviting, it keeps drawing you back for another taste.
Heat Level — 5/10
Clitorch
Chipotle • Tomato
Smoky sophistication with irresistible restraint.
Main Ingredients
Chipotle peppers, slow-cooked tomatoes, aromatic herbs and a touch of tequila.
Sensation
Woody smoke, subtle sweetness and herbal complexity unfold gradually. Elegant rather than aggressive, this sauce prefers a slow, teasing approach and leaves a warm lingering finish.
Heat Level — 2/10
Throat Burner
Poblano Pepper • Tomatillo
Bright green flavor wrapped in smooth sophistication.
Main Ingredients
Roasted poblano peppers, fresh tomatillos, garlic, herbs and citrus undertones.
Sensation
Bright, earthy and unexpectedly smooth. Its personality is flavor-first with only a whisper of heat. Rough like when it's done properly.
Heat Level — 1/10
